REEL

Hold to hook. Release to fly. Climb as high as you can.

REEL is a one-button arcade game about momentum. Hook a glowing anchor, swing around it, let go at the perfect moment and fling yourself up to the next one. Rising lava chases you, red blades block the way, and some anchors crumble when you hang on too long. Climb through four glowing worlds: Dusk Canyon, Glass Deep, Orchid Sky and Moss Spire.

Free to play, supported by ads. No account needed. Made by Odd Theory.
