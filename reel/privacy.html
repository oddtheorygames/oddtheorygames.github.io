Privacy Policy for REEL

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Developer: Odd Theory, oddtheorygames@gmail.com

Summary

REEL is a free game supported by advertising. The game itself does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email, contacts or location. The advertising service it uses (Google AdMob) does collect some device and usage information to show and measure ads, described below.

What the game stores on your device

A small file with your best score, whether sound and vibration are on, and counters used to pace ads and the tutorial. It stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. We cannot see it.

Advertising

REEL shows ads using Google AdMob (Google LLC):

  • Banner ads on the start and game-over screens.
  • Full-screen ads occasionally between runs.
  • Optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch to continue a run.

No ads appear while you are playing a run.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use, according to Google's own policies:

  • your device's advertising ID (you can reset it or opt out of personalisation in your Android settings),
  • device and software information (model, operating system version, language, screen size),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • how ads were shown and interacted with, and diagnostic information.

This is processed by Google, not by us. See Google's policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and AdMob data usage.

Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region where it is required, the game shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later with the "Ad privacy options" button on the start and pause screens, which appears where this is required. If you decline, no personalised ads are requested.

Other things the game does and does not do

  • It does not require an account and does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.
  • It uses the internet only to load ads.
  • It does not access your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • It uses the phone's vibration motor for short haptic feedback (you can switch this off in the game).

Children

REEL is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country) and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this changes, this policy will be updated before the new version is released.
